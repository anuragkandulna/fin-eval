India-Specific Personal Finance Basics

Tax Deductions Under Section 80C (up to Rs 1,50,000):
- PPF contributions
- EPF contributions
- ELSS mutual funds (3-year lock-in)
- Life insurance premiums
- Home loan principal repayment
- Children's tuition fees
- NSC (National Savings Certificate)
- 5-year tax-saving FD

HRA (House Rent Allowance) Exemption:
Salaried individuals can claim HRA exemption for rent paid.
Exemption is minimum of: actual HRA received, 50% of basic salary (metro) or 40% (non-metro), actual rent minus 10% of basic salary.
Requires rent receipts and landlord PAN if annual rent exceeds Rs 1,00,000.

Home Loan Tax Benefits:
- Principal repayment: Section 80C (up to Rs 1,50,000)
- Interest payment: Section 24(b) (up to Rs 2,00,000 for self-occupied property)
- Additional benefit for first-time buyers under Section 80EE or 80EEA

Standard Deduction:
Rs 50,000 standard deduction available for salaried individuals under old tax regime.
Under new tax regime, standard deduction increased to Rs 75,000 from FY 2024-25.

Old vs New Tax Regime:
Old regime: higher tax rates but many deductions available (80C, HRA, home loan interest)
New regime: lower tax rates but most deductions not available
Rule of thumb: if total deductions exceed Rs 3-3.5 lakhs, old regime may be beneficial
Consult a tax professional for personalised advice based on your income and deductions

CIBIL Score and Credit Health:
Score range: 300-900. 750+ considered good for financial approvals.
Factors affecting score: payment history (35%), credit utilisation (30%), length of history (15%), credit mix (10%), new inquiries (10%)
Check score free once per year from CIBIL website or via many banking apps.
Dispute errors in credit report — incorrect entries can unfairly lower your score.
